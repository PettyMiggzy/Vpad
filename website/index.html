How a match works

Four steps from menu to firefight

No download and no sign-up. Open the page, pick a name and go.

1

Pick a side

Two teams, blue and red, each named after a token. Join one and the match fills up with computer players, so it is always on.

2

Load out

Choose a gun, a mod, armour, boots and grenades. Five saved classes let you switch builds in a second. Plan yours in the armory.

3

Fight

Knock the other team out, hold the zone, or play a timed deathmatch where everyone comes back and the most kills wins.

4

Climb

Every kill earns experience. Level up to unlock bigger guns, upgrade them with mods and earn camo for mastering a weapon.

The maps

Big enough to snipe. Tight enough to brawl.

Each map is up to 220 metres long with towers, decks and long lanes, and corners close enough for a shotgun. A bullet from a sniper rifle still carries 240 metres.

Built for variety

Play it your way

Elimination

Best of three rounds. Knock out the other team before they knock out yours.

Hold the Zone

Own the circle in the middle with no enemy inside it to fill your bar, or take them all out.

Deathmatch

Whoever creates the match sets the clock, from 3 to 30 minutes. You respawn after every kill. Most kills when time runs out wins, and a tie plays on to the next kill.

Real sniping

A round scope that sways when you move, a held breath to steady it, and shots that drop damage with distance. Long scopes and steady scopes are mods you unlock.

Fair shots

The server checks every shot and looks back in time to where you saw your target, so a good aim lands even on a slow connection.

Phone ready

Touch controls, a lighter version for phones and a full-screen install on Android and iPhone. Turn the phone sideways and it fills the screen.

The armory

Ten guns. Every one has a job.

From a free pistol to the marksman rifle, each gun wins at its own range. Compare real numbers, plan a build and see what you need to unlock.
