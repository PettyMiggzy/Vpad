Controls

  • Computer: W A S D to move, mouse to aim, click to fire, right click to aim down the sights, R to reload, G for a grenade, F for the knife, Q to swap to your pistol, Space to jump, Shift to sprint (and to hold your breath when scoped), Tab for the scoreboard, Esc to pause.
  • Phone: the left thumb moves (push the stick all the way out to sprint), the right thumb drags to look, and the on-screen buttons fire, aim, reload, throw and jump. Turn the phone sideways.

Something is not working

  • If the game stops while loading, tap TRY AGAIN. A phone on a weak connection sometimes drops a file.
  • If a match says the server is busy, practice matches then run on your own device.
  • If the game is slow, set Graphics to Low in the menu.

Report a problem or ask for data deletion

Write to team@vyrechain.com. Tell us the device, what you were doing and what you saw. For data deletion see the privacy policy.
