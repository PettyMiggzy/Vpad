# Vyre Bad Lands contracts (version 0, testnet only)

`WarheadzLands.sol`: land claims, units, builds, shields, raids, buyback pot and holder claims. `npm ci && npm test` runs 32 tests (21 for the land contract, 11 for the launch split).

The map: a grid fixed by a seed; water and mountains in blocks, coast (beside water) and pass (between mountains) plots are premium (20 USDC). Land comes three ways, each costs the project something: the 24-hour average market cap reaches the claim level; the early deal (taken at launch); or the late deal (raised later, bigger). A deal claim gets one premium plot free. Extra plots touch your own, double in price, and can be bought at any market cap.

`LaunchSplit.sol` / `LaunchFactory`: the launch path that makes a deal unbypassable without touching VyrePad. A launch made through the factory has a small contract as its creator of record in the pad, so the pad pays the creator's tax there. That contract pays the treasury's share first, and has no function to hand the role on, change the pad's tax wallets or lower the share (the share can only be raised; a deal launch also keeps a tax floor, so the share is never over an empty tax). The person keeps the rest and can split it between up to four wallets. The function signatures it calls were compared with the compiled VyrePad ABI and match; it has not been run against a live pad yet.

Rules in code: claim at the 24-hour average market cap; units and defenses burn the nation's coin; USDC only speeds up or buys shield time (60% to the nation's war pool, 40% to the treasury); raids are a fixed formula with no chance; a won raid moves 20% of the loser's war pool into the winner's buyback pot; the buyback burns the winner's own coin; claims are off until the owner switches them on, the creator can route at most half of a win to claims, can only lower it, and never earns points.

Not built yet: the 10M-coin holders' eligibility (needs a hold snapshot per round), trading-activity scoring, the market-cap adapter over the pad's pools, the real buyback helper (a mock stands in), the "different buyers" check (cannot be done on chain).

Known limits, found while writing it: the world-growth rule is written but not tested (it needs hundreds of plots); anyone can still launch on VyrePad directly and skip the factory (those launches get no deal and no land, and keep paying the pad's 1%); forcing every launch through the factory needs a small change inside VyrePad, which reopens its audit; any holder of 0.5% of the supply can use up their nation's raid window with a tiny raid; one person owning two coins can raid themselves to move USDC between their own pots. Both need a fix or a rule before mainnet. Not audited.
