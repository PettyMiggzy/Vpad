# Vyre Bad Lands: Token Arena (3D)

The first-person arena in three.js. The rules (movement, height, shots, grenades, bots, rounds) and the three maps are the match server's own files (`../server/game.js`, `../server/maps.js`), read directly: one copy.

- `npm install && npm run dev` to play against bots; `?a=NAME&b=NAME` sets the two token teams; `?q=low` turns off shadows and glow, `?q=mid` halves the shadow detail.
- Three maps, each with its own sky, light and props, and high decks reached by long ramps (sniper spots): Cargo Yard (dusk, stacked containers, four decks), Dust Town (noon, rooftops and a tower), Refinery (night, tanks and a catwalk across the middle).
- Controls: WASD move, mouse look, click fire, right click zoom (the sniper rifle zooms hard), R reload, G grenade, phone: a stick on the left (push it all the way out to sprint), drag anywhere on the right to look, a big FIRE button you can hold and drag to aim with one thumb, RELOAD, GRENADE and ZOOM (sniper) buttons, and Easy aim (on by default) that lines up shots and fires when you are on target. A how-to card shows the first time, and a prompt asks you to turn the phone sideways. Every weapon has a magazine and a reload time.
- Game types: Elimination, and Hold the Zone (stand in the glowing circle with no enemy in it to fill your team's bar; a full bar wins the round, or knock the other team out). Moves: jump (Space; hop over low cover), knife (F; from behind it kills), swap to the pistol (Q), sprint, health regeneration after five calm seconds, and kill-streak rewards (three kills: a drone shows enemies on the map; five: a speed and reload boost).
- Progression: levels from experience (kills, headshots, knife and grenade kills, round and match wins), weapons and gear unlock by level, every weapon has kill counts and three mastery stars, and gold and obsidian camo are earned. Five saved classes (loadouts) you can name and switch between.
- Shop: ten weapons including a revolver, burst carbine, light machine gun, auto shotgun and marksman rifle; gun mods (bigger or faster magazine, steadier grip, longer barrel, hollow points), armour up to a plate carrier, helmets (cut head-shot damage), boots, grenade kits (more frags, heavy frags) and camo. Play coins only.
- Sound: generated sound files (shots, reloads, steps, impacts, blasts, map ambience) in `public/sfx`, placed in stereo and softened by distance. First-person arms play the real reload and throw animations.
- Speed: soldiers in the crowd use lighter models, soldiers out of view are not drawn or animated, and Graphics (Auto/High/Mid/Low in the menu) steps detail down by itself when the frame rate is poor.
- Soldiers, weapons and map props are generated 3D models: pictures made with the image service, turned into textured models (soldiers rigged on a 24-bone humanoid skeleton with shared animation clips) and compressed (`gltf-transform optimize`, webp 1024, meshopt). Files in `public/models`. A map loads only the props, textures and sky it uses.
- Ground, wall and deck textures and the sky panoramas (which also light the scene) are Poly Haven, CC0 (see `public/CREDITS.txt`).
- Hosted play: when the page can reach a match server (`?server=URL`, or `VITE_SERVER=auto` at build time for the same address the page came from), every match runs on the server (`src/net.js`, `src/netmatch.js`), practice in a private room, challenge in a shared room that waits for a player on each side, and the shop, coins and levels live on the server (`src/online.js`). Without a server it plays offline against bots as before; `?offline` forces that. Setting a server up: `../deploy/README.md`.
- Gun feel (`server/gunfeel.js`, shared by the rules and the page): each weapon has a fixed recoil pattern (the same spray every time, lifts the aim and wanders sideways, settles after the trigger is let go), spread that grows with movement, jumping and a long spray, aiming down the sights (right mouse, or the AIM button on a phone: tighter, steadier, slower, the weapon's own zoom), damage that falls with distance, and leg and head hits. The server holds the truth; the page replays the kick, so a shot goes where the crosshair is. The crosshair widens and tightens with the spread.
- Graphics levels: Ultra (computers only; pick it in the menu) adds ambient occlusion (soft contact shadows), per-map colour grading, a vignette, fine film grain and drifting dust in the light, on top of High (4096 shadows, bloom, 4x smoothing). Auto starts at High on a computer and Medium on a phone and steps down by itself when slow.
- Phones: a lighter set of the same models, textures and sky (`public/models-m`, `tex-m`, `hdr-m`: textures at 512, 256 for crowd soldiers and props, black emissive maps dropped, sky at half size) loads on touch devices or small-memory ones (`?lite=1` forces it, `?lite=0` turns it off). Made with `tools/shrink-models.mjs` (models) and by scaling the texture and sky files. A match holds about 120 MB of pictures in graphics memory on it, against about 770 MB before. The first-person gun models load only for the guns you carry, and one map's pictures are freed when the next map loads.
- Not done: more characters, mobile performance tuning on real phones.
