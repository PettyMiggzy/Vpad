# Style guide

Night arena: dark asphalt floor, concrete walls, glowing trim in lime on the middle walls and in each team's colour near its own base.
Soldiers are blocky, with a vest and a glowing pack in the team colour so friend and enemy read at once from any distance.
UI is HTML over the canvas: lime on near-black panels, bold small caps.
