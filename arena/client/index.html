TOKEN ARENA

Prototype. Two token teams, a top-down map, best of three rounds. Bots fill the empty seats, so a match is always on. Play money only: nothing here touches a wallet.
Earn coins by winning fights and rounds, spend them in the shop. Move: WASD or the left thumb. Aim: the mouse or the right thumb. Fire: click, or push the right thumb out. The server decides every hit.
