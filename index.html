Reading this mock: a coin claims land when its market cap reaches about $50,000 (about $9,200 of USDC bought in net, before fees, by the pool's maths), holds it for 24 hours and has enough different buyers. Building costs USDC, which VYRE keeps, plus the nation's coin, which is 60% burned and 40% added to the nation's war chest, a shared defense fund that pays no one. Wars are scored by trading activity; defenses, the chest and holders' paid reinforcements add a capped bonus, so spending cannot buy a war. A slice of the fees a war creates is its prize and buys back the winner's coin. Skins and banners are cosmetic and paid in USDC. Nobody's liquidity is touched, and nothing here pays out money or promises that a coin goes up. The wording of the buyback and the chest goes to the lawyer before anything real. Every number above is invented for this preview.
